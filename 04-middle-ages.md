# Age 4: The Middle Ages

Rome falls, and Europe breaks into kingdoms ruled from stone castles. Knights ride out, farmers work the land, and cathedrals take a hundred years to build.

The Black Death sweeps through, killing a third of Europe. It's one of the darkest chapters in the log.

But the light never fully goes out. In Baghdad, the House of Wisdom keeps old books alive and adds new ideas to them: algebra, astronomy, medicine. Monks in quiet monasteries copy manuscripts by hand, one letter at a time.

Each careful copy is a backup, so that nothing gets lost forever.
