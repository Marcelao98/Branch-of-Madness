# Age 6: The Industrial Revolution

Steam engines start pounding in England. Factories go up, and cities fill with smoke and workers.

Railways cross continents, steamships cross oceans, and the telegraph sends a message around the world in minutes instead of months.

Then electricity lights up the night, cars replace horses, and two brothers named Wright get a machine to fly. The sky that only saw meteors and birds now sees airplanes.

Change is no longer slow. Every generation lives in a different world from the one before.
