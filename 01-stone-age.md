# Age 1: The Stone Age

The dust settled and the dinosaurs were gone. The tiny furry mammals crawled out of their burrows into a quiet, empty world, and for the first time nobody was trying to eat them.

Sixty-five million years later, one of their descendants stands up on two legs, picks up a rock, and hits another rock with it. The sharp piece that breaks off is the first tool.

Then comes fire. It keeps the cold away, scares off the big cats, and turns raw meat into dinner. Around the flames, people start telling stories, and on cave walls they paint the animals they hunt.

Humanity's first commit: `git init`.
