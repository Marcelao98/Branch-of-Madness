# Age 3: Classical Antiquity

Iron replaces bronze, and empires grow big enough to cover whole maps.

In Greece, philosophers argue in the marketplace about what is true, what is good, and why anything exists at all. Some of them invent democracy; others invent geometry.

Rome builds roads, aqueducts and laws that reach from Britain to Egypt. In China, the Great Wall stretches across the north, and the Silk Road carries goods and ideas between East and West.

It's the first time the world feels connected. Many separate stories start merging into one.
