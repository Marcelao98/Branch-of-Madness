# Age 1: The Long Reign

The orange sky faded, and the world went on exactly as before. No dust, no winter, no extinction. Just another Tuesday in the Cretaceous.

So the dinosaurs kept going, for sixty-five million more years.

The giants slowly disappeared on their own, too big and too slow for a changing climate. The ones that thrived were the small, fast and clever ones: the raptors. Their brains grew a little bigger every few million years. They started hunting in packs with plans instead of instincts, then making sounds that meant specific things, then teaching those sounds to their young.

The tiny furry mammals stayed tiny. They never got their chance. They still live in burrows, waiting, and nobody pays them any attention.

Then, one stormy night, lightning sets a tree on fire. Most of the pack runs. One young raptor doesn't. She steps closer, tilts her head, and carefully picks up a burning branch with her claws.

The dinosaurs have discovered fire.
