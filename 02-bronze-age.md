# Age 2: The Bronze Age

People stop wandering. They plant wheat along the rivers of Mesopotamia and Egypt, wait for it to grow, and build houses so they don't have to leave.

Villages become cities. Someone melts copper and tin together and gets bronze, harder than any stone. Kings appear, then taxes, then the people who keep track of the taxes.

To keep track, they press marks into wet clay. That is writing: the first time a human memory could outlive the human who had it.

From here on, history is no longer just remembered. It is recorded, just like a commit log.
