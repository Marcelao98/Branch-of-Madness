# Age 5: The Renaissance and the Age of Exploration

Europe wakes up. Artists like Leonardo da Vinci and Michelangelo paint and sculpt as if trying to catch up on a thousand years at once.

Gutenberg builds the printing press. Books that took a monk a year to copy can now be made by the hundred. Knowledge stops being a single fragile copy and starts being distributed: everyone gets their own clone.

Ships sail past the edge of the known maps. Columbus reaches the Americas, Magellan's crew sails all the way around the world, and Copernicus moves the Sun to the center of the sky.

The world turns out to be much bigger than anyone thought.
