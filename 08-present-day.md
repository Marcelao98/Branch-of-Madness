# Age 8: The Present Day

It's 2026, and the world is a mess.

People argue every day about whether AI is going to take over the world, take their jobs, or just write their emails. Elections are loud, angry and exhausting, the news is worse, and everyone is tired of living in "historic times".

Somewhere in Brazil, an unemployed engineer sits in front of his computer. He should probably be sending out résumés. Instead, for reasons nobody fully understands (including him), he is using Git to write a story about dinosaurs.

He is not quite sane. He knows it.

He scrolls back through the log: the fire, the clay tablets, the empires, the plague, the printing press, the steam engines, all the way to Linus and his weekend project. Sixty-five million years of commits, all of them stacked on top of one moment.

The meteor.

He stares at that commit for a long time. Then a thought passes through his head, quietly, the way the most dangerous ideas always do:

*What if the meteor didn't fall?*
